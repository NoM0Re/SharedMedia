TeX Gyre Adventor WotLK

Modified builds of TeX Gyre Adventor 2.609 by GUST e-Foundry.

texgyreadventor-wotlk-regular.ttf is derived from texgyreadventor-regular.otf.
texgyreadventor-wotlk-bold.ttf is derived from texgyreadventor-bold.otf.

The CFF outlines were converted to TrueType with fontTools 4.68.0 for WoW
3.3.5a. Glyph mappings, advance widths, and OpenType layout tables were
preserved. The font family is named TeX Gyre Adventor WotLK.

See GUST-FONT-LICENSE.txt for the terms of these fonts. The complete,
unmodified upstream release is available from GUST e-Foundry.
https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/tex-gyre/adventor/tg_adventor-otf-2_609-31_03_2026.zip
